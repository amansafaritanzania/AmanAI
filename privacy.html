Privacy Policy

Effective September 2026

This policy explains how Aman AI handles information used to provide accounts, conversations, memory and AI features.

Information we handle

  • Name, email address, preferred language and account settings.
  • Secure session information, Google account identifier when Google Sign-In is used, and password-recovery records.
  • Chats, saved AI memory, feedback and workspace information.
  • Files or images you choose to upload and information extracted from them for the requested AI task.
  • Limited technical information needed for security, abuse prevention and service operation.

How information is used

We use this information to operate Aman AI, keep accounts secure, provide chat history and memory, process requested AI features, recover accounts and maintain service reliability.

Passwords and recovery

Normal passwords are not stored in readable form. Password-reset codes are temporary and are intended to be stored only in protected form. Successful password resets may invalidate existing sessions.

Google Sign-In

If you use Continue with Google, Google verifies your identity. Aman AI receives information needed to identify the verified Google account, such as the verified email and Google account identifier. Aman AI does not receive your Google password.

Service providers

Aman AI may use third-party providers for hosting, databases, authentication, email delivery, AI processing and optional media features. Information necessary to perform a feature may be processed by those providers under their own terms and privacy practices.

Chats, memory and uploads

Chats and saved memory may remain connected to your account so they can be available across sessions. Do not upload information you do not have permission to share.

Young users

Users should follow the age and parental-consent rules that apply where they live. Where parent or guardian permission is legally required, the service should only be used with that permission.

Changes

This policy may be updated as Aman AI changes. The effective date will be updated when appropriate.

This is a practical starter privacy policy for Aman AI. Before a larger commercial launch, it should be reviewed against the laws that apply to the business and its users.
